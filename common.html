Common methods

The package supports both directed (prefix D) and undirected graphs (prefix U), and the classes for these share a common interface.

The graph classes represent a single graph, either directed or undirected, and they share Graph:

The vertices of both kinds of graph are Vertex objects. A vertex has no direction of its own: the graph it belongs to decides how an edge is recorded in the edge lists of its vertices. Edges are represented by the Edge class, which is used by both directed and undirected graphs.

The graph classes share almost their entire interface, defined once on their common base class Graph. This section documents that shared interface by task rather than by class; see Construction and basic usage for the DGraph and UGraph classes and Edges & Vertices for vertices and edges.
